# Contact 4 Asset Licenses

## Lush Meadow Ground v2

- Project-authored, AI-assisted ground texture created specifically for the Contact 4 scene
- Used as the diffuse layer over the CC0 Leafy Grass PBR normal, roughness, AO and displacement maps

## Dandelion Meadow Ground v3

- Project-authored, AI-assisted dense dandelion meadow texture created specifically for the Contact 4 scene
- Used as the diffuse field layer beneath the scanned CC0 dandelion canopy

The assets in this directory are downloaded from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) and released under the [CC0 1.0 Universal license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
